## What we've heard so far

**The problem as stated by the project:**
People served by the Salvation Army Belkin Communities of Hope can't reliably reach essential services like hospitals and shelters. Booking a ride takes digital access, such as a phone, data or an email address, and many of them don't have it. Staff arrange transportation on their behalf with partner organizations, sometimes free and sometimes paid.

**The human cost, in the team's words:**
- **Frustration**
- **Anger**
- **Loss**, named most specifically as **lost time**

**Our reflection:**
"Lost time" is the thread to pull on. For someone without stable housing, income or a phone, time isn't neutral. A missed or late ride can mean:
- a missed medical appointment that takes weeks to rebook
- a shelter bed given to someone else
- a missed court date, benefits appointment or job shift
- hours waiting at a front desk without knowing whether anyone is coming

Lost time also falls on staff, whose phone calls and follow-up take time away from being present with people, and on partners and drivers, who lose time to no-shows and unclear pickups.

## Listening guide for Alvin and Belkin staff

Use this in a 30–45 minute conversation. Ask one question at a time and let silences sit. Write down exact words where you can.

### The people
1. Tell me about the last person you helped get to an appointment. What happened, start to finish?
2. Tell me about a time a ride *didn't* work out. What did it cost that person?
3. Who are the people that never even ask for a ride, because they've given up?

### The work today
4. Walk me through how you arrange a ride right now. Who do you call? How long does it take?
5. Who are the partners you rely on? What can each one do, and what can't they?
6. How do you decide between a free ride and a paid one? Who approves the money?
7. How do you know a person actually arrived? What happens when they don't?

### The deeper causes
8. Why do you think this has been hard to fix for so long?
9. What existing options (HandyDART, transit passes, taxi vouchers, volunteer drivers) have you tried? Where do they fall short?
10. Is there anyone who benefits from the current way staying the way it is, or who would resist it changing?

### Hope
11. If this were fully solved a year from now, what would be different for the people you serve? For your staff?
12. What would make you *not* want to use a tool for this?

### If possible: someone who has needed a ride
Only with Alvin's guidance, consent, and a staff member present.
- What's it like when you need to get somewhere important?
- What helps? What makes it harder?
- Is there anything you'd want the people arranging rides to know?
